FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you are looking for? Message us and we will reply quickly.

What services does RWD Designs offer?+

Logo design, taglines, branding and brand guidelines, visiting cards, letterheads and stationery, website design and development (HTML, Node.js, WordPress and CMS), e-commerce, domain and hosting, SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads and social media.

How long does a logo or website take?+

A logo and stationery set usually takes 3–4 working days. A business website also usually takes 3–4 working days once we have your content and images.

Do you build with WordPress or custom code?+

Both. WordPress or another CMS is ideal when you want to edit content yourself. Custom HTML or Node.js is best for unique features, web apps or maximum speed. We recommend the right fit for your goals.

Can you handle my domain and hosting?+

Yes. We register your domain, set up secure hosting with SSL and create professional email addresses on your domain, then keep everything maintained.

How does pricing for Google Ads and Meta Ads work?+

You pay us a monthly management fee for strategy, setup, creatives and reporting. Your advertising budget is paid directly to Google or Meta, so you always see exactly where it goes.

Do I own the final files?+

Yes. Once the project is complete and paid, you receive all final and source files — logo vectors, print-ready stationery and full access to your website.
